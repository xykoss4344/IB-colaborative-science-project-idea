THE SEEBECK EFFECT & CARNOT THERMOPHILE LIMIT

Thermoelectric Semiconductor Physics Model

Thermoelectric Generators (TEGs) operate solid-state conversion by converting thermal temperature gradients directly into electrical energy via the **Seebeck Effect**.

Seebeck Voltage Output \[V = S \cdot \Delta T = S \cdot (T_h - T_c)\] Where \(V\) is the generated voltage, \(S\) is the material Seebeck Coefficient, \(T_h\) is the hot-side temperature, and \(T_c\) is the space cold-sink.
Carnot Efficiency Limit \[\eta_{\text{max}} = 1 - \frac{T_c}{T_h}\] Defines the maximum theoretical thermodynamic efficiency of the TEG converter operating between the Earth and deep space.
